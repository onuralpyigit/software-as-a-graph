\subsection{Research Questions}
\label{sec:exp:rqs}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Diagnostic Ranking Efficacy):} How does the RM composite's rank correlation with cascade failure impact compare to single-metric structural baselines (betweenness, degree)?
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Catalog Screening and Calibration):} How well does the anti-pattern catalog's component-level agreement match its precision/recall profile, and what accounts for its implication rate?
    \item \textbf{RQ3 (Attribution Validity and Substrate Adequacy):} Does the Availability dimension carry genuine signal on the layer this evaluation measures, how does stratification by node type affect the pooled ranking result, and how does this re-measurement compare to our prior published claim?
    \item \textbf{RQ4 (Prescriptive Refactoring Efficacy \& Operator Survival):} Does the closed-loop prescriptive engine achieve statistically significant reductions in System Risk Index across heterogeneous scenarios, and how do individual refactoring operators survive counterfactual verification?
    \item \textbf{RQ5 (Verification Yield, Compositionality \& CI/CD Feasibility):} What proportion of generated candidates survive verification, do individually admitted edits compose beneficially, and what is the execution latency of diagnostic gating and prescriptive verification across system scales?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Experimental Protocol and Metrics}
\label{sec:exp:protocol}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Detection Metrics:} Spearman rank correlation $\rho(Q, I_{\text{comp}})$ (pooled and stratified), Cohen's $\kappa$, NDCG@10, Top-$k$ overlap, precision, recall, and $F_1$ against simulation ground truth $I_{\text{comp}}$ (Section~\ref{sec:catalog:validation}). Baselines: betweenness and degree centrality. For $Q(v)$, betweenness, and degree, the predicted and true critical sets are both sized by the same box-plot-or-top-20\% rule (Section~\ref{sec:results:detection}), which pins precision, recall, and $F_1$ to near-equal values by construction; the catalog's flagged set has no such constraint, so its precision and recall are measured independently.
    \item \textbf{Prescriptive Metrics:} System Risk Index ($\mathrm{SRI} = 0.5(1-H_R) + 0.5(1-H_M)$), $\Delta\mathrm{SRI} = \mathrm{SRI}_{\text{baseline}} - \mathrm{SRI}_{\text{mutated}}$, per-operator candidate generation counts, admitted edit counts, and per-edit survival rates.
    \item \textbf{Verification Parameters:} $\kappa = 1.0$, $\Theta = \{0.1, 0.2, 0.5\}$, $S = \{42, 123, 456\}$.
    \item \textbf{Two Oracles, Not One:} This paper's ground truth, $I_{\text{comp}}(v)$, is an independent discrete-event cascade simulator modeling message dropouts and queue exhaustion under component removal on the directed \texttt{DEPENDS\_ON} projection. Our prior work \cite{yigit2025graph} validated against a different oracle, Reachability Loss, on the undirected pub-sub graph---a distinction Section~\ref{sec:results:attribution} returns to when reconciling the two papers' correlation figures.
\end{itemize}
