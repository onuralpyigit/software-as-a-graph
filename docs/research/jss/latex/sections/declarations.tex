\section*{Data availability}
The seven synthetic scenario datasets, their generator configurations, and the manifest of canonical
dataset hashes are included in the replication package, from which every synthetic dataset
regenerates byte-identically. The three real-world architecture graphs of Section~\ref{sec:7.1} and
their adapter are included on the same terms. Result artifacts are provided for the in-distribution
evaluation (Table~\ref{tab:18}), the sensitivity sweeps of Section~\ref{sec:8.3}, the edge-removal
measurement of Section~\ref{sec:8.2}, the remediation sweep of Section~\ref{sec:6.7}, and the three
real-world validation runs of Section~\ref{sec:8.5}. \textbf{One exception is recorded rather than
glossed:} the per-fold artifact behind the Leave-One-Scenario-Out results of Table~\ref{tab:20} was
not retained and cannot be regenerated from the archive without re-running the sweep;
Section~\ref{sec:8.1} and Section~\ref{sec:9.2} state what follows from that. A link to the archived
package will be supplied on acceptance.
