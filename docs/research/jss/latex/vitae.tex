\section*{Vitae}

\noindent\textbf{Ibrahim Onuralp Yigit} is a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of
Computer Engineering at Istanbul Technical University, where his doctoral research
addresses graph-based modelling and analysis of distributed publish--subscribe
systems. His interests span software architecture analysis, architecture-level
dependability, cascading-failure prediction, and graph representation learning.
With Feza Buzluca he published a graph-based dependency analysis method for
identifying critical components in distributed publish--subscribe systems at IEEE
RASSE 2025. For the present work he led the conceptualization, methodology,
implementation, validation, and analysis.
