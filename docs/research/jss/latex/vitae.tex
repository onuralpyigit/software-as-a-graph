\section*{Vitae}

\noindent\textbf{Ibrahim Onuralp Yigit} received his B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Engineering from Istanbul Technical University (ITU), where he is currently a Ph.D. candidate. His doctoral research addresses graph-based modelling and analysis of distributed publish--subscribe systems. His research interests span software architecture analysis, architecture-level dependability, cascading-failure prediction, and graph representation learning. With Feza Buzluca he published a graph-based dependency analysis method for critical-component identification in publish--subscribe systems at IEEE RASSE 2025. For the present work he led the conceptualization, methodology, implementation, validation, and analysis.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Feza Buzluca} received his B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Engineering from Istanbul Technical University (ITU). He is currently a Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at ITU, where he supervises doctoral and graduate research in software engineering. His principal research interests include software architecture, object-oriented analysis and design, distributed computing systems, and dependable software engineering. He has led multiple national research projects and published extensively in software engineering journals and conferences. For the present work he contributed the conceptualization, methodology, validation, and critical review.
