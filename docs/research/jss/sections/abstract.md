# Abstract

Publish–subscribe systems decouple components in space and time, which also hides how failures propagate. We present Software-as-a-Graph (SaG), a pre-deployment analysis framework that converts architecture descriptions into typed multigraphs with Quality-of-Service (QoS) weighted dependencies and ranks components by cascading-failure impact, without runtime telemetry. SaG combines a training-free closed-form engine, graph-learning engines with 16-dimensional QoS edge encodings, and hybrid engines where a learned model corrects the closed-form score, plus an ISO/IEC 25010 attribution layer explaining why a component is critical. Under leave-one-scenario-out cross-validation over twelve synthetic architectures, SaG’s QoS-aware projection improves ranking over unweighted centrality on every held-out architecture (Spearman $\rho = 0.553$ vs. $0.349$). The learned engine alone is statistically on par with this closed-form engine, and a capacity- and channel-matched control shows that the QoS edge encoding, not relation-specific weights, drives its accuracy. The hybrid engines are the most accurate on architectures resembling the training distribution ($\rho = 0.657$ and $0.683$), outperforming the closed-form engine on 11 of 12 held-out folds, each under a decision rule registered before its run (Holm-adjusted $p \le 0.0068$; nominal, since folds share training data). Pure learned engines transfer better to substantially different systems: on hand-authored models of five open-source systems they rank zero-shot at $\rho = 0.760$–$0.805$, against $0.511$–$0.526$ for every training-free score and $0.662$–$0.695$ for the hybrids, and roughly double top-$K$ critical-set overlap. Neural inference takes milliseconds. QoS-aware typed graphs thus give pre-deployment dependability analysis a strong foundation, and combining learned and closed-form reasoning is most accurate on familiar architectures.

**Keywords:** Graph neural networks; dependability; publish–subscribe; cascading failures; hybrid learning; quality of service; explainable AI
