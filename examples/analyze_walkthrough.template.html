Analyze Walkthrough

Analyze measures position and nothing else. For every component it computes a vector of structural metrics on the dependency graph: how much leans on it, what it sits between, how redundant its surroundings are. It assigns no score and makes no judgement; those come later and read from this vector. This page lets you look at the metrics one at a time on a 15-component system.
