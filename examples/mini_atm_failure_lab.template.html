Mini-ATM Failure Lab

Four simulators, one 15-component air-traffic system. Pick a component to fail and see what each engine does with it. Every number on this page is the output of the repository's own engines run on this topology.

FaultInjector · I*(v) · training labels

Who starves once the failure has cascaded?

The cascade runs in waves. In each wave a topic's loss is the worse of its publisher loss and its router loss, scaled by a QoS factor. A subscriber whose mean feed loss reaches 0.2 may fail, which silences whatever it publishes in the next wave. The score is the mean final feed loss over the four subscribers.

FailureSimulator · I_comp(v) · validation oracle

What does the system as a whole lose?

Each component carries an impact between 0 and 1. Hosts pass their impact to residents, libraries to users, publishers to topics. Redundancy is a minimum: a topic takes the least impaired of its brokers, a subscriber the least impaired of its feeds. A logical hop fires with probability equal to the edge's QoS weight. The score then combines four system-wide losses.

injected failed (impact 1) degraded (impact below 1) badge = cascade depth
MessageFlowSimulator · I_dyn(v) · research probe

How much less traffic gets through over time?

This engine runs 60 simulated seconds. Publishers emit at each topic's declared rate, subscribers queue and serve messages, and the component fails at 30 s. The score is the delivery rate before the fault minus the rate after it. Healthy demand here is 74 deliveries per second: 40 + 30 + 2 + 2.

ChangePropagationSimulator · I_M(v) · maintainability

If its interface changes, who has to adapt?

This is a development-time question, not a runtime failure. The engine first re-derives dependency arcs from the raw edges, then walks them backwards from the changed component. Every direct dependent is reached. The walk continues through a dependent only if the coupling weight is at least 0.20 and the dependent's instability is at least 0.20.

Side by side

Four engines, four different rankings

The scores live on different scales and answer different questions, so only their rankings are comparable. Radar tops I* and I_dyn yet sits at the bottom of I_comp, because its only topic carries little QoS weight. Display scores zero on I* as a pure sink, yet 0.296 on I_comp, because three of the eight delivery paths end there.

How these numbers were produced. FaultInjector with the five recommended seeds over every node type. SimulationService.run_failure_simulation_exhaustive("system") on a MemoryRepository, plus one single run for the library. MessageFlowSimulator with duration 60 s, fault at 30 s, seed 42, full QoS and target utilisation 0.65. ChangePropagationSimulator on the dependency view that FailureSimulator builds.

All options are the published defaults. A different run seed can change which probabilistic hops fire in the FailureSimulator runs. The topology is a hand-made demo (examples/mini_atm.json) and is not part of the scenario corpus. Regenerate this page with python examples/run_simulator_walkthrough.py.
